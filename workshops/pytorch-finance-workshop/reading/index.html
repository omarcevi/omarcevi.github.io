Further Reading and Resources

Verified core resources to go deeper into what we covered:

  1. Official PyTorch documentation and tutorials:
    • PyTorch Tutorials — Learn the Basics: Tensor, Dataset, DataLoader, nn.Module and the training loop.
    • PyTorch nn.LSTM reference: input and output tensor shapes (batch_first=True) and the gate equations.
    • PyTorch nn.BCEWithLogitsLoss reference: numerical stability and the built-in sigmoid in binary classification.
  2. Time-series validation (walk-forward) and data leakage:
    • Scikit-Learn — TimeSeriesSplit: the official explanation of why standard k-fold cross-validation does not work for time series, and of expanding-window (walk-forward) validation.
    • Scikit-Learn — Common Pitfalls and Recommended Practices (Data Leakage): how to avoid leakage between training and test data in preprocessing steps such as StandardScaler.
    • Hyndman & Athanasopoulos — Forecasting: Principles and Practice (Evaluating Forecast Accuracy): the standard reference on naive (random-walk) baselines and time-series error metrics.

Final reminder: This workshop and guide are for education only and are not investment advice.
